🧠 Concept Breakdown

Making Decisions with if, elif, and else

💡 Everyday Analogy:
Conditionals are like fork in the road in a video game. IF you choose left door, path A happens. ELIF you choose right door, path B happens. ELSE, something unexpected happens!

Syntax Cheat Sheet:

# Making choices in Python
choice = input("Do you open the RED door or BLUE door? ").lower()

if choice == "red":
    print("🔥 A friendly dragon gives you a golden key!")
elif choice == "blue":
    print("🌊 Water floods the room! You swim to safety.")
else:
    print("❓ You stood still for too long and a ghost scared you away!")
